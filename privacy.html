PumpSaver privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

PumpSaver shows fuel prices at UK petrol stations near you. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and it does not collect personal data.

Location

If you allow it, PumpSaver reads your device's location to work out how far each station is from you. This happens on your device. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. If you refuse, the app shows stations around central London instead.

What the app downloads

The app downloads one file of fuel prices from GitHub Pages. The same file is sent to every user, and the request contains nothing about you. Like any website host, GitHub may log your IP address when the file is downloaded; see the GitHub privacy statement.

What is stored on your device

Your chosen fuel type and sort order, and a copy of the latest prices so the app works offline. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Directions

Tapping Directions opens your maps app or browser at Google Maps with the station's location. What happens there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Data source

Prices come from the UK government's Fuel Finder scheme. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. PumpSaver is independent and not affiliated with the UK government.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
